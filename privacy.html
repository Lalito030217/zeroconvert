Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

ZeroConvert ("we", "our", or "us") operates a browser-based file conversion utility engineered with a strict zero-upload architecture.

1. Local Browser Processing

All file conversions (including PNG, JPG, WEBP images, PDF merging, and audio/video extraction) take place 100% locally inside your web browser using client-side technologies. Your files are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any external server.

2. Cookies & Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use third-party analytics and advertising partners, including Google AdSense, to display ads and analyze site traffic. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@zeroconvert.it.com.
