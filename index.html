Why Zero-Server Conversion Matters for Privacy

Most online file converters require you to upload your personal photos, financial PDF statements, or private recordings to a remote cloud server before processing them. Even when platforms claim to delete files after a few hours, your data still travels across external networks and resides temporarily on third-party infrastructure.

ZeroConvert eliminates this risk entirely. Built on modern HTML5 Canvas, Web Audio API, and client-side WebAssembly PDF libraries, every conversion executes exclusively inside your browser's local memory. Your files never leave your computer or smartphone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my files ever uploaded to a cloud server?

No. You can even disconnect your internet connection after loading this page and every conversion tool will continue working normally. Zero bytes are transmitted over the network.

Is there a maximum file size limit?

Because there are no server bandwidth costs or upload queues, ZeroConvert imposes no artificial file size caps. Conversion speed and capacity depend strictly on your device's available RAM and processor.

Why convert images to WEBP format?

WEBP reduces image file sizes by 25% to 35% compared to traditional JPEG and PNG formats while preserving sharp visual detail, making it ideal for websites, portfolios, and faster page loads.

Can I merge password-protected PDF files?

Encrypted PDFs cannot be parsed without unlocking them first. Remove password restrictions from your PDF files prior to merging them locally.
