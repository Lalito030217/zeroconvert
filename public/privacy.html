Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • Domain: zeroconvert.online

1. Zero-Server File Processing

ZeroConvert (zeroconvert.online) is engineered around a strict client-side privacy model. All file conversions—including image format transformations (PNG, JPG, WEBP), PDF merging, and audio extraction—are executed entirely inside your web browser's local memory using client-side Web APIs.

We never upload, transmit, store, or inspect your files on any external server. Your documents, images, and media remain exclusively on your own device at all times.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require user accounts, registration, or personal identification to use our utilities. However, like most websites, basic technical data may be processed automatically:

  • Local Preferences: Your selected interface language is saved locally in your browser via localStorage so the site remembers your preference on future visits.
  • Standard Hosting Logs: Our hosting provider (Vercel) may temporarily log standard HTTP request metadata (such as anonymized IP addresses, browser type, and page response status) strictly for security, DDoS mitigation, and performance monitoring.

3. Cookies and Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

To keep ZeroConvert free to use, we display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks, including Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to ZeroConvert and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Children's Privacy

ZeroConvert does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Policy & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technical or regulatory changes. If you have any questions about this policy, please reach out via our Contact page.
